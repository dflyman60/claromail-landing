How it works

Four passes in Scan, then you decide what leaves the mailbox.

  1. Scan Group messages by sender, find already-blocked leftovers, move them to Deleted Items, then see who you’ve emailed — without copying mail out of Microsoft.
  2. Review & clean Delete, block, or delete + block by address or entire domain. Keep the senders you still want.
  3. Recover or keep Restore mistakes from Deleted Items, or add domains like adobe.com to a Keep list so they stay out of future scans.

We don’t store your emails

ClaroMail sorts and cleans inside your Microsoft account. We don’t copy messages to our servers, and we don’t keep a mirror of your inbox.

  • Mail stays with Microsoft Scan, soft-delete, and block run through Microsoft Graph on the mailbox you sign into — not a ClaroMail mail store.
  • No message archive on our side We don’t upload or retain your email bodies. Tokens and your Keep list stay on this device in Chrome storage.
  • Soft delete by design Cleanup moves mail to Deleted Items. Block rules live on Microsoft’s servers and keep working when the extension is closed.
